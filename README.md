# My Favorite Anime

## Attack on Titan

**Attack on Titan** is a Japanese anime about humans fighting against giant creatures called Titans.

The story follows **Eren Yeager**, who dreams of killing all the Titans and protecting humanity.

I like this anime because of its:
- Amazing story
- Exciting action scenes
- Interesting characters
- Unexpected plot twists

It is one of my favorite anime because it is exciting and full of surprises.
